Controls:
Keyboard:
A D to move left and right
W and S to move up and down respectively while on a rope
Q to throw torch
E to display torch parabola
SPACE to jump while on the ground or on a rope
Xbox Controller (only supported for gameplay, working on the menu scenes):
Left Joystick to move
X to throw torch
Y to display torch parabola
A to jump
Back button for exiting a level
