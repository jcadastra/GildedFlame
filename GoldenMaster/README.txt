Since last time, programmers created 15 new finalized levels, all playtested. Furthermore, we implemented a new game-feature of rain, that extinguishes all flame that is hit by the rain, allowing us to create new sagas dedicated around this rain feature. UX-wise, we have produced new sounds for player actions and full screen. We also revamped our UI, providing the user relevant information whenever they lose / win. Transitions between levels have also been implemented, allowing a seamless transition between levels, death screens, and victory screens. Finally, we have implemented a save feature that allows the game to track the progress of the user, not only forcing players to pass previous levels to access the next, but also allowing the user to automatically load into the latest level they were playing in. For full immersion, we have implemented sound effects for player actions, monster actions, and environmental movement. Visually, we have implemented full screen and upped the brightness of the lights to give the user more information and reduce the need for “blind throws” or “blind jumps, “ as well as indicators for hidden monsters. Throughout this process, we were constantly testing out new interactions with existing features, playtesting, level creating, and fixing bugs as they came.

Controls:
Keyboard:
A D to move left and right
W and S to move up and down respectively while on a rope
Q to throw torch
E to display torch parabola
SPACE to jump while on the ground or on a rope
ESCAPE to exit level
Xbox Controller (only supported for gameplay, working on the menu scenes):
Left Joystick to move
X to throw torch
Y to display torch parabola
A to jump
Back button to exit a level
